# Customer Journey Map — Person 2

| Stage | Behavior | Thought | Emotion | Pain Point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Payment Arrives (Unpredictable Timing) | Client payment received, amount varies | "I don't know when the next one will come" | Uneasy | Irregular Income Mismatch |
| 2. Spending Decision | Spends it quickly, doesn't think about saving | "I'll use it while I have it" | Automatic behavior | Unsustainability |
| 3. Low-Income Month | No or very little income comes in | "How will I manage this month?" | Worried | Irregular Income Mismatch |
| 4. Moment of Awareness | Thinks "I should have had an emergency fund" | "Why did I never start" | Regretful | Meaninglessness + Unsustainability |
| 5. Trial and Abandonment | Tries an app, fixed-date system doesn't work, quits | "This isn't for me" | Frustrated | Irregular Income Mismatch |

## Moment of Truth
Stage 3 — the panic felt during a low-income month. A flexible,
income-sensitive system could prevent this moment entirely. This is the
key product opportunity.
