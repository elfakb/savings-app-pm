# Affinity Mapping → Pain Points

Raw insights were grouped into thematic clusters to identify core pain points.

## Cluster 1: Lack of Visibility
Insights: 1, 2, 12
Pain Point: Users can't clearly see where their money goes or how their
savings are progressing.

## Cluster 2: Trust & Knowledge Gap
Insights: 3, 8, 16
Pain Point: Financial products (funds, forex, tax mechanics) feel complex
and untrustworthy; users avoid action out of fear of making the wrong choice.

## Cluster 3: Perceived Meaninglessness of Small Amounts
Insights: 6, 7
Pain Point: Users believe saving small amounts is pointless, which
prevents them from starting at all.

## Cluster 4: Sustainability / Discipline Issue
Insights: 4, 10, 11
Pain Point: Users start saving but abandon the system at the first
difficulty (emergency, lack of time); systems remain one-off, never
becoming a habit.

## Cluster 5: Irregular Income vs Fixed Systems
Insights: 14, 15
Pain Point: Fixed-date, fixed-amount saving models don't work for users
with irregular income.

## Cluster 6: Goal Ambiguity
Insights: 13, 17
Pain Point: Users know they "should" save but lack a concrete goal,
leading to an intention-action gap.

## Secondary Observations (context, not primary drivers)
- Onboarding friction (Insight 9): Complex sign-up flows lose users early.
- Social/emotional dimension (Insight 5): Financial struggle isn't openly
  discussed, reinforcing isolation rather than solution-seeking.

## Final Pain Point List
1. Lack of Visibility
2. Trust / Complexity
3. Perceived Meaninglessness
4. Unsustainability
5. Irregular Income Mismatch
6. Goal Ambiguity
