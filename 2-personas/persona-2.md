# Persona 2 — Irregular-Income Independent Worker

**Age:** 26
**Status:** Freelance graphic designer, previously a part-time student
**Income:** Highly variable month to month

## Behavior Summary
When money arrives, she tends to spend it quickly because she doesn't
know when the next payment will come. Fixed-date automatic saving
systems don't work for her since some months bring no income at all.
She knows she needs an emergency fund but has never managed to start one.

"Some months I earn a lot, some months nothing — setting a fixed date
just doesn't make sense for me."
