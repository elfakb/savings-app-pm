# Persona 1 — Fixed-Income Young Professional

**Age:** 24
**Status:** Recent graduate, 8 months into first job
**Income:** Fixed monthly salary

## Behavior Summary
His paycheck arrives regularly, but by the end of the month he doesn't
know where it went. He wants to save, but the investment/fund options
in his banking app feel complex and risky, so he never touches them.
He keeps postponing with "I'll start next month."

Financial security, a sense of control over his money.

concrete goal.
