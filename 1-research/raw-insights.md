# Raw Insights (Simulated Interviews)

Note: This is a simulated/practice case study created for portfolio purposes.
Insights below are constructed based on realistic behavioral patterns from
secondary research on youth financial behavior, not live interviews.

## Profile 1: Recent Graduate (Fixed Income)
1. "My paycheck lands and I feel fine for a week, but by week 3 I have no idea where it went." (No spending visibility)
2. "My bank app has a 'savings' tab but I've never opened it, I don't know what it does." (Low awareness)
3. "Investment funds, forex, all of that feels complicated and risky, I'm afraid of losing money." (Trust/knowledge gap)
4. "I actually want to save but I keep telling myself 'not this month, next month.'" (Procrastination)
5. "My friends are in the same boat, nobody talks about it, it feels almost shameful." (Social stigma)

## Profile 2: University Student (Part-time)
6. "I don't have much money left to save anyway." (Perceived meaninglessness of small amounts)
7. "Even if I saved 50 TL, it feels pointless, why bother?" (Motivation gap)
8. "My parents say 'save money' but never taught me how to start." (Financial literacy gap)
9. "I tried an app but it asked for membership/limits, I gave up." (Onboarding friction)

## Profile 3: Early-Career Employee (1-2 years, mid income)
10. "I set up an automatic transfer once but pulled it all out during an emergency, never set it up again." (Sustainability issue)
11. "I tried tracking in Excel but stopped after 2 weeks, too time-consuming." (Manual tracking not sustainable)
12. "I'd like to see where my savings are going and how they're growing, but no app shows this clearly." (Visibility/progress gap)
13. "I don't really have a goal, I just think 'I should be saving' without a clear target." (Goal ambiguity)

## Profile 4: Freelancer / Gig Worker (Irregular Income)
14. "Some months I earn a lot, some months nothing, picking a fixed day makes no sense for me." (Fixed systems don't fit irregular income)
15. "When money comes in, I spend it fast because I don't know when the next payment will arrive." (Uncertainty-driven spending)
16. "There are taxes and deductions too, I can't even calculate exactly what I take home." (Added financial complexity)
17. "I know I should have an emergency fund, I just never started." (Intention-action gap)
