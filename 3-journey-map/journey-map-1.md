# Customer Journey Map — Person 1

| Stage | Behavior | Thought | Emotion | Pain Point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Payday | Salary lands, feels relieved | "I'll save this month" | Hopeful | — |
| 2. First 1-2 Weeks | Normal spending, no attention paid | "I still have time" | Neutral | Lack of Visibility |
| 3. Mid-Month | Opens bank app, checks fund/investment tab | "What do these terms mean, is it risky?" | Anxious | Trust / Complexity |
| 4. End of Month | Balance is low, unsure where money went | "I didn't save again" | Disappointed | Visibility + Goal Ambiguity |
| 5. Postponement | Says "I'll start next month," takes no action | "Starting now won't change anything anyway" | Resigned | Goal Ambiguity |

## Moment of Truth
Stage 3 — the moment he views fund/investment options. This is where he
either decides to "try it" or "gives up." This is the key product
opportunity.
